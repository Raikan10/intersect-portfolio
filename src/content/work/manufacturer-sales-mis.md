## The challenge

A high-volume catalogue manufacturer with several plants and a large export business. Sales data lived in software built decades ago. Leadership wanted unit-wise sales, drilling down to product and customer, without waiting on manual reports.

## What we did

We did not start with a strategy deck. We asked for one month of sales data, built the plant to product to customer drill-down, and set it up so people could ask for a chart in plain language, including on their phones.

The demo ran six days after the first meeting.

## What came out of it

The room went straight from the demo to three concrete workstreams:

1. Sales and purchase reporting that answers questions in plain language.
2. Export paperwork across many markets, drafted automatically for a person to check.
3. A daily report of alerts on what was missed in email.

## What is still open

The next step is reading the old system's source code to confirm exactly which tables and fields the old system relies on. A reporting layer is only useful once its numbers reconcile with the source, so that reconciliation is the gate for everything else.
