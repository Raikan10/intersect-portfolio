## The challenge

A fast-growing retail chain selling several global electronics brands. Store operations, finance, brand claims and stock ran across seven systems that did not share data. Staff bridged the gaps by hand every day, including re-keying sales into brand visibility portals.

The instinct was to buy an ERP. The risk was automating a process nobody had written down.

## What we are doing

Ajay works on this from the client side as head of IT, alongside an external process consultant.

- An as-is study of finance, vendor management, brand operations, stores and service, through interviews with the people who do the work.
- A to-be design and the requirements an ERP would need to meet, written before talking to vendors.
- A data layer that pulls from the existing systems, so reporting and AI tools do not wait on the ERP decision.
- Store-facing tools built Tamil-first, because that is the language the work happens in.

## What we have learned so far

Giving the IT team better AI tools did not change output on its own. The constraint was the organisation: who owns which data, who approves what, and where work waits. That is the part being fixed first.
