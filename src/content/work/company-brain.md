## The challenge

Ajay runs operations and IT across a distribution business, a retail chain and several AI projects at once. Commitments made in one meeting were getting lost by the next. Notes lived in five places and nobody, human or AI, could see the whole picture.

## What we built

A wiki in plain markdown with one page per person, company, project and topic. A written schema tells AI agents how to file things. Every day, agents read new meeting transcripts, journal entries and email, update the relevant pages, record decisions with their sources, and flag commitments that have gone quiet.

Because it is plain text with explicit links, the same knowledge base works for a person reading it in a notes app and for an agent answering a question.

## Why it matters for clients

A business that writes down how it works is a business agents can help run. Most companies hold this knowledge in people's heads and WhatsApp threads. The first step of a company brain is not software. It is agreeing what gets written down, by whom, and where.

## What we would do differently

Start smaller. The first version tried to capture everything. The useful core turned out to be people, open commitments and decisions, each with a source.
